## Reading

The laboratory can store, and list, three cost pools: supplies and services, salaries, and reagent costs. Promedios Pacientes/Pruebas is an average that relates patients or tests to those costs. The formula is not shown.

This is not the patient price list. Patient prices are Lista de Precios under Reportes, with columns Particular, Aliados, Hospitalizado, Emergencia, and Estatal. See [lista-de-precios.md](lista-de-precios.md).

The public catalog never mentioned cost accounting. Salaries and reagent costs are now named commands. No amount, period, or allocation rule was on screen.
